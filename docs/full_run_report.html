Methods

Data

  • Reviewed (Swiss-Prot) proteomes from UniProt REST: E. coli K-12 (taxid 83333) and B. subtilis 168 (taxid 224308).
  • GO annotations from QuickGO (ECO:0000269 descendants), filtered to the experimental GO evidence codes EXP, HDA, HEP, HGI, HMP, HTP, IDA, IEP, IGI, IMP, IPI. NOT qualifiers were removed.
  • 24438 annotation rows kept for 4123 proteins; GO graph from go-basic.obo.

Sequence similarity

  • DIAMOND makedb + blastp --very-sensitive, e-value ≤ 1e-3.
  • Outfmt 6: qseqid, sseqid, pident, evalue, bitscore; best HSP per pair.

Embedding similarity

  • facebook/esm2_t6_8M_UR50D; mean-pooled residue embeddings excluding special tokens; sequences truncated to 1022 residues.
  • Rows L2-normalised; cosine similarity matrix; top-5 matches per A protein.
  • Embedding-only candidate = no DIAMOND hit and cosine ≥ 0.90.

Evaluation

  • GO terms propagated over is_a and part_of.
  • Jaccard similarity of propagated term sets; Resnik reported in pairs_scored.tsv as a secondary metric. A sensitivity analysis repeats the comparison after removing the three GO root terms.
  • Two-sided Mann-Whitney U tests vs the random control (seed 42); Cliff's delta effect size; Holm correction across the two tests.

Limitations

  • Mean pooling collapses per-residue information; functional sites may be diluted. Attention pooling or per-domain embeddings could improve signal.
  • Truncation at 1022 residues can drop domains from long proteins (~4123 annotated proteins; see embedding metadata for truncation counts).
  • Sparse experimental annotations: B. subtilis has far fewer experimental GO annotations than E. coli, so most cross-organism pairs are unscorable. This reduces power and can bias the scored subset.
  • GO graph effects: propagated ancestor sets share generic terms; Jaccard is sensitive to annotation depth and aspect composition.
  • DIAMOND is not an exhaustive homology detector; distant homologues may be missed, which is exactly the hypothesis, but it also means some embedding-only pairs are genuine remote homologues.
  • Random controls are sampled from the full proteome cross product, not matched for abundance, length or amino-acid composition.
  • One model, one cutoff, one seed: results may not generalise; larger ESM-2 models and sensitivity analyses are warranted.
